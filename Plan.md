You are a senior UI/UX designer and Flutter expert.

Analyze my Flutter developer portfolio app and provide a full design audit.

## What to audit:

### 1. Visual Hierarchy
- Is the most important info (name, role, CTA) immediately visible?
- Are font sizes creating a clear H1 > H2 > Body structure?

### 2. Color & Contrast
- Is there enough contrast between text and background?
- Is the color palette consistent throughout?
- Does it reflect a Flutter developer's identity?

### 3. Typography
- Are fonts readable and professional?
- Is font pairing working well?
- Are font sizes appropriate for mobile screens?

### 4. Layout & Spacing
- Is spacing consistent (8pt grid)?
- Are elements properly aligned?
- Does it feel cramped or too empty anywhere?

### 5. Navigation
- Is the nav clear and accessible?
- Does it work well on mobile?

### 6. Each Section Check:
- Hero: Strong first impression? Clear CTA?
- Skills: Easy to scan? Shows Flutter expertise?
- Projects: Do they look professional? Enough info?
- About: Engaging or just a text wall?
- Contact: Easy to find and use?

### 7. Missing Elements
- What important sections are missing?
- What info should be added?

## Output format:
For each issue found, give:
❌ Problem: [what's wrong]
📍 Location: [which section]
✅ Fix: [exactly how to fix it]

Then give a final priority list of top 5 fixes to do first.
